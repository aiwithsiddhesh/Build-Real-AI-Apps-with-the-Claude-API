## Returns Policy Overview

PageTurner Books offers a 30-day return policy on all eligible items from the date of delivery. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused and in the same condition that you received it. Items must be in the original packaging where applicable.

## Physical Book Returns

Physical books may be returned within 30 days of delivery provided they are in their original condition — unread, unmarked, and undamaged. Books that have been annotated, highlighted, or have broken spines are not eligible for return. To initiate a return, contact HelpBot or email returns@pageturner.com with your order ID and reason for return. We will provide a prepaid return shipping label within 1 business day.

## Digital Book and eBook Returns

Digital books and eBooks are non-refundable once downloaded or accessed. If you have purchased a digital book but have NOT yet downloaded or opened it, you may request a cancellation within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund. Once a digital file has been accessed, all sales are final. This policy applies to all formats including PDF, EPUB, and MOBI files.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your book arrives damaged or defective, PageTurner Books will replace it at no cost or issue a full refund — your choice. Please contact us within 7 days of delivery. You will be asked to provide a photo of the damage. We cover all return shipping costs for damaged or defective items. Damage categories include: torn covers, water damage, missing pages, printing defects, and incorrect items shipped.

## International Orders

International orders are eligible for returns under the same 30-day policy, however the customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is damaged or defective. Refunds are issued in the original payment currency. Please allow 5–10 business days for international return processing. Customs and import duties are non-refundable.

## Return Timeframes and Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will process your refund within 3–5 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Store credit is available as an alternative and is processed within 1 business day. Exchanges are processed within 2 business days of receiving the returned item. You will receive an email confirmation at each stage of the process.

## Contact and Escalation

For return requests, contact HelpBot in this chat or email returns@pageturner.com. For urgent issues or escalations, call 1-800-PAGETURN (Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm EST). For order tracking issues, use your order ID (format: PT-XXXX) in this chat. Our team aims to respond to all email queries within 24 hours on business days.
